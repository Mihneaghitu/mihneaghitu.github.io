Mihnea Ghitu

PhD Student in Trustworthy AI · Department of Computing, Imperial College London

Me by Regent's Canal

I am a PhD student in Trustworthy AI in the Department of Computing, Imperial College London, supervised by Dr Matthew Wicker. My research asks how we can obtain provable guarantees about the behaviour of machine learning models, from simple neural networks to vision models and large language models. I work on certification of safety properties, including poisoning robustness, explainability, differential privacy, unlearning and more.

Before my PhD, I completed an MEng in Computing (Machine Learning) at Imperial, graduating with First Class Honours. My master's thesis studied the robustness–privacy trade-off in Bayesian neural networks and was selected as a Distinguished Project.

Alongside research, I have industry experience as an Applied Scientist Intern at Entrust, working on synthetic data generation for face matching, an Electronic Trading Developer Intern at Arctic Lake Technologies, where was involved in creating pricing models and financial data platforms, and a Software Development Engineer Intern at Amazon, where I built large-scale Attribute-Based Access Control Systems.

Feel free to reach out at mihnea.ghitu20@imperial.ac.uk.
